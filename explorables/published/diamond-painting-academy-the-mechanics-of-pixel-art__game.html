Level 2: The Physics of the Tray

Gems are poured chaotically. To pick them up, they must be faceted-side up. We use a grooved tray and mechanical agitation to exploit the gem's geometry.

Why it works: The V-shape of the groove forces the gem to flip flat-side down when agitated laterally.

TASK: Shake the tray to align the gems.

Level 3: Adhesion vs. Friction

Plastic gems are non-magnetic. To lift them, we use a hollow brass tip and pink wax. This relies on adhesion overcoming gravity.
