# Explorables from Video

Small experiments turning YouTube videos into interactive essays.
Generated automatically; structure varies by run.

## Examples
- **The Geometry of Packing**  
  Rolling vs folding as volume, void space, and time.
- **The Algorithm of Salsa**  
  Rhythm, weight transfer, and timing as parameters.
- **The AI Profit Paradox**  
  Incentives, power laws, and why failure dominates.

## Notes
- These are not summaries.
- Interactions are inferred, not scripted.
- Specs are sometimes generated first, sometimes not.
